# PRD Quality Review — ProjectOS Validation Build

## Overall verdict

The polished PRD is decision-ready, strategically coherent, and safe to source-extract for downstream UX, architecture, epics, and stories. The prior critical authority contradiction, all three high findings, both medium closeout findings, and the final low copy-acceptance ambiguity are resolved; no rubric findings remain.

## Decision-readiness — strong

The document now states its authority chain directly (§0), and addendum §8 follows rather than contradicts it: the Codex spike is historical rejected evidence, Epic 2 is provider-free, Epic 3 owns provider implementation, and no new Codex authorization work is permitted. Consequential choices are explicit throughout: one local Qualified Adapter Combination opens the experiment, all four adapter targets complete provider scope, negative thesis evidence stops unnecessary adapter breadth, external processing is optional and disclosed, and accepted state remains user-governed (§§4.5, 7.1–7.2; addendum §§5, 8).

The six Open Questions are genuinely unresolved, assigned, and tied to a decision point (§9). None silently reverses a settled direction. In particular, runtime/model qualification and OpenRouter model/cost policy remain implementation-bounded questions rather than excuses to represent unqualified combinations as supported.

## Substance over theater — strong

The vision remains specific to governed semantic continuity rather than generic local AI, project containers, or memory (§1). The single named primary user and four journeys constrain the actual proposal-review, supersession, re-entry, and recovery interactions (§2); there is no persona furniture. Provider, integrity, privacy, deletion, and explainability requirements name concrete product boundaries and failure consequences (§§4.5–4.7, 5), while addendum §§3–5 preserve measurement mechanics and market-derived differentiation that directly affect the experiment.

The new readiness and usability material is also mostly earned. `Qualified Adapter Combination` prevents nominal support claims; SM-3A prevents a small accurate proposal set from hiding material omissions; SM-C4 makes local setup burden visible; and NFR-10/NFR-12 turn prior adjectives into observable checks (§§3, 5.4, 8; addendum §§3.2, 3.4–3.5).

## Strategic coherence — strong

The feature arc still follows one thesis: selected evidence becomes reviewed Change Proposals, accepted changes become governed Canonical State, and that state enables a faster and more trustworthy return (§§1, 4.1–4.4). Success metrics test first value, re-entry, continuity quality, correctness, completeness, and guidance; integrity gates and counter-metrics reject corruption, maintenance theater, engagement volume, blind acceptance, and hidden setup burden (§8).

Yesterday's provider-breadth finding is resolved. PRD §7.1 now separates the product-learning cut line from provider-scope completion, requires one qualified local combination for experiment start, and stops additional adapter work after a negative thesis result unless evidence isolates the adapter as the cause. Addendum §5.1 makes clear that this is a learning boundary rather than a hidden adapter hierarchy.

## Done-ness clarity — strong

All 18 FRs carry concrete consequences, including state-preserving rejection, exactly one Governing Decision after supersession, truthful missing Provenance, reversible correction, offline re-entry, explicit transmission, combination-scoped readiness, recoverable export, bounded deletion, and fake-versus-production contract evidence (§4). The prior SM-3A gap is resolved with minimum evidence volume, an 85% initial-proposal threshold, zero critical omissions, insufficient-evidence handling, and an explicit rethink/stop rule (§§8.1, 8.4; addendum §3.2). NFR-10 now has workload, latency, percentile, and trial bounds; NFR-11 requires history/evidence to remain collapsed or secondary and prevents historical items from sharing current-state affordances or visual priority; NFR-12 has a representative state suite and explicit pass/fail content rules (§5.4; addendum §3.4).

The former NFR-11 ambiguity is resolved without changing its intended outcome. NFR-12 now uses the explicit three-sentence limit and content-suite checks without adding an unbounded sentence-length adjective.

## Scope honesty — strong

The package is candid about users it excludes, product and provider non-goals, commercial deferrals, the one-combination experiment cut line, full provider-scope completion, and the distinction between target status and qualified support (§§2.3, 6–7; addendum §§5–7). Open questions and assumptions have owners and revisit conditions, and the new validation thresholds are presented as personal-validation assumptions rather than universal truth (§§5.4, 8–10).

The prior assumption-round-trip finding is resolved. Six inline assumptions have six semantically matching §10 entries: SM-1, SM-4, SM-3A, NFR-10, NFR-12, and SM-C4. The new entries carry the full sample sizes, thresholds, zero-critical-omission rule, owners, and revisit conditions used inline.

## Downstream usability — strong

The package is now cleanly extractable. The glossary defines the new `Qualified Adapter Combination` term and distinguishes target, support, and readiness (§3). UJ-1 through UJ-4, FR-1 through FR-18, and NFR-1 through NFR-13 are unique and contiguous; success/counter-metric IDs are unique and their FR/NFR references resolve. Each User Journey has the named protagonist Wouter, and feature sections name the journeys they realize.

Most importantly, yesterday's critical downstream contradiction is removed. Addendum §8 now points Architecture to the current four-target spine, explicitly labels Codex evidence historical and non-authorizing, assigns provider-free foundations to Epic 2, and assigns shared provider implementation and qualification to Epic 3. A downstream workflow can follow the handoff without reintroducing excluded Codex work.

## Shape fit — strong

The shape fits a solo personal experiment that is also chain-top. It avoids a synthetic persona set but retains four load-bearing journeys because proposal control, decision supersession, re-entry, and recoverability are meaningful UX flows (§2.4). Capability-grouped FRs, cross-cutting NFRs, explicit experiment metrics, provider-scope cut lines, and a supporting addendum provide enough rigor for UX, architecture, and story creation without turning the PRD into an implementation design.

The added detail remains proportionate to the risk. Provider qualification, omission measurement, setup burden, and local responsiveness are the exact areas where an underspecified personal build could create false validation evidence; their inclusion is earned by the PRD's experimental and downstream role.
